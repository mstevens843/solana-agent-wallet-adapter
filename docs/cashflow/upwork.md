## Screening Rules

Bid when:

- Client has $1k+ budget or clear hourly range above $60/hr.
- Job mentions wallet, mobile, MCP, AI integration, dApp, Solana, production bug, app store, or API integration.
- Client has spend history or the issue is tightly scoped.
- The task can become a paid diagnostic or fixed sprint.

Skip when:

- "Need whole app built" with tiny budget.
- Memecoin volume/sniper/market manipulation.
- Client asks for private keys, botting, or fake activity.
- Job has 50+ proposals and no strong niche fit.
- Client has no concrete issue, no repo, no budget, and no urgency.
